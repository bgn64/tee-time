Feed card redesign — swipeable content

The header and footer stay put. The middle content band swipes between Summary · Scorecard. Its height is locked to the taller pane, so the card never jumps as you move between panes; the shorter pane is vertically centred. Per-hole detail is no longer its own pane — instead, tap any hole-number pill on the scorecard (or the caption beneath it) to open the hole sheet, then swipe / use ‹ › to move between holes. The old segmented tab selector is gone — navigation is the swipe, the minimal dots at the bottom, and (on desktop only) the faint edge arrows.
