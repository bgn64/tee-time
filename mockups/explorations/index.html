Tee Time — five ways it could look

Five bias-free visual directions for the golf app, each a self-contained mobile mockup of the two signature screens: the social Feed and the live Scoring view. Every screen is driven only by the backend data model — handles & avatar colors, course snapshots, stroke vs. scramble, per-hole scores, FIR / GIR / putts stats, and likes & comments — with no reference to the current UI.

Tap a preview to open it full-size. Built to render offline (system font stacks, inline SVG).
