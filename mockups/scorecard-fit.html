Scorecard — fit to the content width

Each scorecard is one nine (Front 9 / Back 9 are separate content sections). The grid is sized to fill the content-section width exactly — hole columns flex (minmax(0,1fr)) and the label/total columns are trimmed — so it never needs its own horizontal scroll. A left/right swipe then always switches sections instead of fighting a grid scroll. Toggle Fixed (today) to see the overflow that causes the conflict (the grid scrolls inside the dashed boundary).

Tip: in Fixed mode, try dragging the grid left/right inside the dashed box — that scroll is exactly what steals the swipe today. In Fit mode there's nothing to scroll.
