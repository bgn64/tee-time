Tee Time – Empty States & Color Schemes

Part 1: Ideas for filling space with 1-2 players. Part 2: Alternative color palettes applied to the scoring and home screens.

Filling Empty Space (1-2 Players)

When there are only 1-2 players, the accordion takes up much less vertical space. Here are ideas for what can fill that space meaningfully.

💡 Idea 1: Inline Mini-Scorecard

Show a condensed scorecard grid below the accordion — hole numbers across the top, scores filled in as you go. This gives at-a-glance context without needing a separate screen. With 1-2 players there's plenty of room for it.

💡 Idea 2: Round Stats / Running Summary

Show live stats: total score vs par, fairways hit, putts (if tracked later), best/worst hole so far. Grows richer as the round progresses. Feels like a "dashboard" below the scoring area.

💡 Idea 3: Larger Hole Illustration

Expand the hole header area — bigger badge, more hole info (yardage, handicap index if available), maybe a simple par-indicator visual (dots representing strokes). The scoring area expands vertically to breathe more.

💡 Idea 4: Adaptive Chip Size

When fewer players are present, make the score chips larger and more spaced out. The accordion body grows to fill available space. Bigger tap targets and a more relaxed feel.
